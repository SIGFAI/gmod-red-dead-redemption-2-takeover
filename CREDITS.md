# Credits

All art, sound and music in this mod were generated for it with fal (nothing is copied from Red Dead Redemption 2 or any other game):
- Wanted poster, plank texture, tumbleweed texture, dirt road texture: fal image / texture models (Nano Banana 2, z-image turbo tiling), background removed with BiRefNet v2.
- Revolver shot, harmonica sting, Dead Eye heartbeat, horse neigh, spurs and saloon piano music: fal sfx / music models.

The 3D models come from Garry's Mod and Half-Life 2 themselves (Hunter blocks, citizens, metropolice, horse statue, cactus, gravestones, explosive barrels), used as stock props. Names of characters are original jokes.
All Lua code is original.
